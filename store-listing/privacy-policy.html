ZQ Text Sandbox 8F4K2 Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-16

ZQ Text Sandbox 8F4K2 V1.0 processes text locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not transmit text, license keys, device identifiers, or analytics data to a server.

A purchased license is stored locally on the device to unlock Pro features. Removing the local app data removes that activation record.
